# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home and office users who need to scan paper documents quickly into local PDFs, with optional upload to Paperless-ngx. They are usually focused on a practical task: confirm the scanner is available, choose a sensible destination, scan, and trust that the document was saved.

## Product Purpose

Nospe is a compact Windows desktop scanning utility for an Epson DS-410 workflow. Its purpose is to make scanning feel predictable and low-friction: one clear primary action, visible scanner and backend state, understandable defaults, and reliable feedback about saved files and Paperless upload results.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Nospe should feel like a dependable desktop tool, not a flashy marketing product or a generic admin template. The interface can be restrained, but it should communicate confidence in the physical scan workflow.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic shadcn demo surfaces that look interchangeable with any settings app. Avoid Epson Scan 2-style legacy density, mystery states, and technical jargon. Avoid decorative dashboards, heavy color, and modal-heavy flows that interrupt a straightforward scan task.

## Design Principles

- Make trust visible: scanner state, destination, progress, and final save location should never feel hidden.
- Keep the main path obvious: the user should know within seconds whether they can scan and what will happen next.
- Put defaults in service of speed: common home and office scans should require little adjustment.
- Reveal complexity only when needed: Paperless, profile, and advanced scan settings should not compete with the scan action.
- Preserve desktop reliability: compact layout, keyboard access, clear focus, and durable error recovery matter more than visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard operation across the primary scan flow and settings sheet. State changes such as loading, success, warning, and error should be perceivable beyond color alone and announced where appropriate.
